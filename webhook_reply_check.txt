Q: What is the most famous dish at U Fleku?
A: U Fleků (או פלֶקוּ)’s most famous dish is the “Golden Vltava Dumplings” — dumplings filled with caramelized duck. They’re served only on Tuesdays, so it’s best to arrange your visit in advance.
